# T-015 · Regime-aware strategy (Backtest Epic)

**Epic:** Backtesting & Monte Carlo · **Owner:** coder → sr-dev review · **Status:** To Do

## Goal
Build a regime-aware strategy that can actually *beat* buy & hold on the down-legs — not just ride the gold bull like 12-1 momentum did (T-013's HOLD verdict, edge −0.00%, is exactly the failure mode this card exists to fix). Long when gold is above its 200-day trend **and** real rates are falling; flat otherwise. The whole point is to capture upside while standing aside on down-moves so it can produce a positive edge vs buy & hold instead of matching it.

## Deliverables
1. `backtest/strategies/regime_aware.py` — the regime filter:
   - **Trend leg:** long only when price > 200-day moving average (or another documented trend proxy). Flat below it.
   - **Macro leg:** stay long only while real rates are falling; flatten when they rise. Real rate = nominal rate − breakeven inflation (or a documented proxy) — pull point-in-time, never look-ahead.
   - Signal is the AND of both legs: `long` when both true, `flat` otherwise. No shorting in v1 (matches Phase 0 long-only default).
2. **Baseline comparison** — strategy vs buy & hold on identical dates and costs (T-011/T-013), so beating the baseline is explicit. This is @user's core ask: see buy & hold performance versus what we build.
3. Report integration — regime exposure (% time in market), edge vs buy & hold, and the Buy/Hold/Sell verdict at report end (T-013).

## Acceptance / sr-dev checks
- Every decision sees only point-in-time data (T-012 integrity gate must pass; injected-future-row tests prove no leakage on both legs).
- Regime filter is deterministic and documented — trend proxy, real-rate source/proxy, and the AND logic are all specified in the card or code docstring.
- Baseline comparison renders: strategy vs buy & hold per name/window with beat/miss explicit.
- Strategy produces a *positive* edge vs buy & hold on at least one down-leg window (the whole point — if it still just matches buy & hold, the regime filter isn't doing its job).

## Notes / constraints

- The regime filter's exit-on-down-leg behavior should be testable in isolation: a synthetic down-trend window must show the strategy flat while buy & hold is underwater.
